# RC turbine-jet speed records and Mach-1 attempts

Compiled 2026-09-13. Every figure comes from a page fetched in this session (URLs given). Mach 1 at sea level is about 1225 km/h (761 mph), as quoted in the DroneXL article below.

## 1. Is there an FAI record for RC turbine jets? No.

- The FAI Sporting Code Section 4 (Aeromodelling Records, 2022 edition, Table 1 "Class F") lists radio-control record categories only for **piston-motor** aeroplanes/seaplanes/helicopters (F3), **gliders**, and **electric-motor** models (F5: S / SOL / COMB). The only jet or reaction-motor record category is **F135, control-line (tethered) speed**. There is **no FAI record category for radio-controlled turbine-jet models**.
- The general rules for open records are: flying weight including fuel of 5.00 kg or less, piston swept volume of 10 cm3 or less, pulse-jets only for control line (F135), and wing area of 150 dm2 or less.
- For straight-line speed records the course is flown in both directions, and above 300 km/h only automatic timing (accurate to 1/100 s) is allowed.
- Source: https://www.modelaircraft.org/sites/default/files/events/Records/sc4_vol_ciam_records_22.pdf (FAI document hosted by the AMA; the fai.org copy returned 403).
- So the reference records for RC jets are **Guinness World Records** entries with documented timing, listed below.

## 4. Mach-1 attempts and projects with small (<50 kg) air-breathing non-rocket aircraft

**No documented flight has happened.** The Boom Prize page (2026) states: "no radio-controlled airplane has ever flown supersonic under air-breathing power."

## 5. Design-relevant takeaways (from the sources above)

- The verified RC-jet ceiling is about **749 km/h (~Mach 0.61 at sea level)**, set by a 7.5 kg aircraft on a Behotec 180 (40.5 lbf, about 180 N, per The Drive). That works out to a static thrust-to-weight of about 2.4 (my calculation: 180 N / (7.5 kg x 9.81)).
- Commercial kit jets limit speed well below that. The BVM Bandit manual says "Limit Maximum Speed to 200 MPH" (322 km/h). Elite Aerosports advertises its BDXL as exceeding 200 mph.
- AMT rates its Olympus HP / Titan / Nike engines for "Maximum operating speed 1000 km/h" and 8000 m altitude (spec sheets in `microturbojet_database.csv`). That is still subsonic, below the Boom Prize requirement.
